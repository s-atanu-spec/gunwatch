# Initial news relevance review

Reviewed on 27 September 2026. All 99 stored headlines and available RSS summaries were inspected manually by the assistant. This is a relevance audit, not independent fact-checking or full-article verification.

- 77 shooting/gunfire incident reports.
- 10 follow-up/legal/memorial reports.
- 10 explicitly non-US reports.
- 2 generic or insufficient firearm evidence records.

Syndicated copies remain as source records. The reading desk groups identical normalized headlines and retains their links; this is coverage grouping, not proof that different stories concern one incident. Strict incident matching remains separate.

Every decision and source link is recorded in NEWS_REVIEW.json. Missing summaries and unresolved locations remain explicitly labelled. US edition settings do not constrain incident geography. The revised query uses gun-specific phrases and exclusions; title-level foreign-place checks and review decisions supply a second filter. Uncertain geography remains marked for review.
